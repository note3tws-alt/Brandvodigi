Kannur district is several markets, not one

A campaign that works in Kannur town can fail in Iritty or Payyanur. Before we spend a rupee, we decide which towns, which buyers and which radius matter for your business.

  • Kannur town (Thana, Fort Road, Caltex, South Bazaar): the district's busiest retail and services market. Shoppers compare on Instagram and Google before they visit, so reviews, photos and a clear offer decide who gets the call.
  • Thalassery and Kuthuparamba: strong food, bakery and family-business culture. Reputation and repeat customers drive growth; content that shows people and craft works better than discount posts. See our Thalassery page.
  • Taliparamba: a trading and education hub on the national highway. Retailers, coaching centres and clinics here draw customers from surrounding villages, so radius targeting has to be wider than you might think. See our Taliparamba page.
  • Payyanur and Pilathara: close-knit towns where trust and WhatsApp conversations close the sale. This is our home market. See our Payyanur page.
  • Mattannur: the Kannur International Airport has brought hotels, travel, real estate and new retail to the area. Many buyers here are first-time visitors or Gulf travellers, so Google Maps visibility matters more than social reach.
  • Iritty and the hill towns: plantation, trading and hill-route businesses towards the Karnataka border. Smaller audiences mean every rupee of ad spend has to stay inside the right radius.

Seasons matter across the district. Onam, Vishu, Eid, Christmas, the wedding season and the Theyyam season (roughly October to May in North Malabar) change what people search for, where they travel and what they buy. We plan content and budgets around them instead of posting the same thing every week.

What usually holds Kannur businesses back online

These are the patterns we see most often when a Kannur business asks us to look at their marketing:

  1. No Google Business Profile, or one with five reviews and no photos. For "near me" and "in Kannur" searches, the map listings get most of the attention. A good profile and a steady review routine is often the cheapest win available. Our Google Business Profile guide explains how.
  2. Boosting posts instead of running campaigns. Boosts are optimised for likes and reach, not enquiries. A proper Meta Ads campaign with a lead form or WhatsApp button, tracked properly, costs the same and tells you what each enquiry cost.
  3. Ads shown to the whole district. A salon in Thalassery does not need customers in Cherupuzha. Tight radius targeting by town cuts waste quickly.
  4. English-only content. English builds credibility, but Malayalam captions and voiceovers usually get the response. Most of our Kannur campaigns run in both.
  5. No way to tell which enquiries came from marketing. If calls and WhatsApp chats are not tracked, nobody knows what is working. We set up tracking before we scale spend.

How a Kannur engagement starts

  1. A strategy call (free): we look at your Google listing, your social pages and any ads you have run, and tell you plainly what we would change first.
  2. A 90-day plan: which towns, which services, what budget, and what a good enquiry is worth to you.
  3. Set-up: tracking for calls, WhatsApp and forms; Google Business Profile fixes; first content shoot.
  4. Launch and weekly testing: we test offers, creatives and audiences, and move money to what brings enquiries.
  5. A monthly report in plain language: enquiries, cost per enquiry, what worked, what we will change.

You own your ad accounts, pages, website and creative files from day one.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have an office in Kannur town?

No. Our base is in Payyanur, Kannur district. We meet clients across Kannur district in person and work with them online day to day.

Which towns in Kannur district do you serve?

All of them, including Kannur town, Thalassery, Taliparamba, Payyanur, Mattannur, Iritty, Kuthuparamba, Panoor and Pilathara. We target campaigns by town so you only pay to reach people who can actually become customers.

What budget do I need for Meta Ads in Kannur?

It depends on your goal and market size. We recommend a short test budget after the strategy call, show ad spend separately from our fee, and only scale what brings enquiries. Our digital marketing cost guide explains how budgets are usually set.

Can you get my business into the Google Maps results in Kannur?

We can make your Google Business Profile as strong as possible: correct categories, services, photos, posts and a routine for genuine reviews. Nobody can guarantee a map position, because Google also weighs distance from the searcher, but a complete, active profile with real reviews gives you the best chance.

Do you build websites for Kannur businesses?

Yes, conversion-focused websites and landing pages, built for mobile and connected to WhatsApp and call tracking. See website design.

Is it better to start with SEO or ads?

Ads bring enquiries within weeks; SEO and Google Maps take months but keep working without paying per click. Most Kannur businesses do best starting with ads and a Google Business Profile, then building SEO alongside. Our SEO vs Meta Ads vs Google Ads guide compares them.
