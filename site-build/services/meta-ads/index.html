Services

Facebook and Instagram Ads Built for Cost-Efficient Leads

Brandvo Digital Co. is a Meta Ads agency in Kerala that plans, creates and manages Facebook and Instagram campaigns for leads, bookings and sales. Every campaign starts with what a customer is worth to you, and every report shows what results cost.

Why most Meta Ads in Kerala waste money

  • Boosting posts instead of running campaigns. The boost button optimises for likes, not enquiries.
  • Targeting too wide. A Payyanur shop advertising to all of Kerala pays for people who will never visit.
  • One creative, no testing. Ad fatigue sets in within weeks; costs climb quietly.
  • No tracking. Without counting calls, WhatsApp chats and form leads, nobody knows which ad worked.

What's included

  • Audience testing: location radius, interests, lookalikes and retargeting.
  • Creative testing: several hooks, formats (Reels, static, carousel) and offers in Malayalam and English.
  • Lead and sales campaigns: instant forms, WhatsApp click-to-chat, landing pages or website sales.
  • Tracking: Meta Pixel and conversion events, plus lead counts from WhatsApp and calls.
  • Weekly optimisation and a monthly report in plain language: spend, leads, cost per lead, what we changed and why.

You own the ad account, the page and every creative.

Who it works best for

Real estate developers and land sellers, clinics, salons, restaurants and bakeries, showrooms, education and coaching, gaming and entertainment venues, and service businesses that sell through calls or WhatsApp.

Results from Meta Ads clients

  • SAJ Realty: "Brandvo Digital Co. helped us get highly qualified leads for our real estate properties. We now spend our time speaking to people who are genuinely interested."
  • Playbox Club, Kochi: "Brandvo Digital Co. helped us get steady PS5 sales."

How a campaign runs

  1. Strategy call: offer, audience, budget and what a lead is worth.
  2. Setup: tracking, audiences, landing page or WhatsApp flow.
  3. Creative: 4 to 8 ad variations to test.
  4. Launch and test for the first weeks.
  5. Scale what works, cut what doesn't, report monthly.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I spend on Meta Ads?

Enough to test several ads properly. We recommend a test budget after the strategy call based on your market and goal; ad spend is paid directly to Meta and shown separately from our fee.

What is a good cost per lead in Kerala?

It varies widely by industry, offer and location. We set a target from your margins and track it every week rather than quoting a number before testing.

Facebook Ads or Google Ads?

Meta Ads create demand and work well for visual, local and impulse offers; Google Ads capture people already searching. Read our comparison: SEO vs Meta Ads vs Google Ads.

Do you make the ad creatives?

Yes. Our team writes, designs and edits the ads, including Reels.
