Paid Media Tied to Revenue, Tested Every Week

Brandvo Digital Co. is a performance marketing agency in Kerala for businesses that want every rupee of ad spend tied to leads, bookings or sales. We plan, run and measure paid campaigns from our base in Payyanur for clients across Kerala and the Gulf.

Performance marketing, plainly

You pay for advertising, we measure what it brings back, and we move budget towards what converts. That needs three things most campaigns skip: proper tracking, constant testing and honest reporting.

Boosting a post asks Meta for likes. A performance campaign asks for a business action, such as a WhatsApp enquiry, a booked site visit or an order, and is judged on what that action costs.

Why ad spend gets wasted

The same problems turn up in most ad accounts we are asked to look at:

  • No tracking. Nobody knows which ad brought which customer, so the budget is set by gut feel.
  • One ad, running for months. Creative wears out, costs rise, and nothing new is tested.
  • Leads that never get a reply. Enquiries sit in a Meta inbox for a day; by then the customer has called someone else.
  • Reporting on clicks. Click-through rate looks fine while sales stay flat.

Fixing these usually matters more than finding a clever audience.

What's included

  • Conversion tracking: Meta Pixel and conversion events, form, WhatsApp and call tracking, and a simple lead sheet your team can update.
  • Weekly testing: audiences, creatives, offers and landing pages, one change at a time so we know what worked.
  • Funnels and landing pages: built to turn clicks into enquiries, with the WhatsApp button where people expect it. See lead generation.
  • Creative production: ad Reels, statics and carousels in Malayalam and English, refreshed before they wear out.
  • ROAS and cost-per-lead reporting: what you spent, what came back, what we will change next.
  • Budget planning: a channel plan and budget split for the next 90 days.

How a campaign runs

  1. Numbers first. We ask what a customer is worth to you, your margins and how many enquiries your team can handle. That sets a realistic target cost per lead.
  2. Tracking set-up. Pixel, events and a lead sheet before any money is spent.
  3. Launch with several angles. Different offers, hooks and audiences, so the data shows a winner quickly.
  4. Weekly optimisation. Cut what doesn't convert, put more money behind what does, and write new creative.
  5. Monthly review. Cost per lead, cost per sale where you can tell us, and the plan for next month.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between performance marketing and boosting posts?

Boosting optimises for engagement. Performance campaigns optimise for a business action (a lead, a booking, a purchase) and are measured on what that action costs.

How quickly can I see results?

Campaigns usually produce first results within weeks; reaching a stable, efficient cost per lead takes a few rounds of testing.

What budget do I need to start?

Enough to give each test a fair chance. For most local businesses in Kerala that is a modest daily budget for the first month, separate from our fee. We'll suggest a figure from your numbers on the strategy call.

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. Ad spend is paid to Meta from your own ad account, so you keep control and full visibility. Our management fee is separate.

Do you guarantee ROAS?

No honest agency can. We set targets from your numbers, report against them every month and tell you plainly what's working.

Can you track WhatsApp enquiries?

We track clicks on WhatsApp buttons and click-to-WhatsApp ads, and a simple lead sheet records which chats became customers.
