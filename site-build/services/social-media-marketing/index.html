Social Media Marketing That Brings Enquiries, Not Just Likes

Brandvo Digital Co. is a social media marketing agency in Kerala that plans, creates and manages Instagram and Facebook for businesses that want customers, not just followers. We are based in Payyanur, Kannur district, and work with shops, clinics, salons, builders and service businesses across the state.

Why most business pages don't bring customers

Most Kerala businesses post something. Few get enquiries from it. The usual reasons are easy to spot once you look:

  • Posting without a plan. A festival greeting one week, a stock photo the next, then nothing for a month.
  • Content about the business, not the customer. "We are the best" posts get scrolled past. Posts that answer a buyer's question get saved and shared.
  • No route to buy. Interested people can't find prices, location or a WhatsApp button, so they move on to a competitor who replies first.
  • Measuring the wrong thing. Followers and likes go up while the phone stays quiet.

Social media works when it is treated as part of the sales process: content that builds trust, a clear way to enquire, and someone who replies quickly.

Two services, one goal

Social media management keeps your brand consistent and active: a content calendar, regular publishing, design, captions and community replies.

Social media marketing grows the audience that buys: growth strategy, engagement plans, creator collaborations and audience building, connected to Meta Ads when you want to scale faster.

Most clients start with management and add marketing once the page looks credible enough to send paid traffic to.

How we plan content for Kerala audiences

Built around how your customers decide

A salon customer wants to see real results and the price range. A real estate buyer wants location, approvals and a walkthrough. A bakery customer wants to see the cake they'll order tonight. We start from those decisions and work backwards to the posts.

Malayalam, English, or both

Many Kerala businesses do best with a mix: Malayalam voice-overs and captions for local reach, English for professional services and Gulf-based audiences. We test both and keep what gets enquiries.

The Kerala calendar

Onam, Vishu, Eid, Christmas, the wedding season, school reopening and Gulf holiday season all change what people buy. We plan campaigns weeks ahead, not the night before.

Reels first

Short-form video reaches people who don't follow you yet. We write hooks and scripts, guide the shoot (on your phone is fine) and edit for completion, not just views.

Influencer marketing that sells

For Unicorn Bakes in Payyanur, local food and lifestyle creators introduced the shop to people who already trusted them. That creator content was then reused in ads. Together with consistent content, Meta Ads and branding, the business made ₹95 lakh in sales from ₹8 lakh in ad spend over two years (figures reported by the client). We pick creators on audience fit and location, not follower count.

Who it suits

Social media marketing works best for businesses whose customers browse before they buy: restaurants and bakeries, salons and boutiques, clinics, real estate, education, gyms and gaming, interior and furniture stores. If your buyers mostly search on Google with urgent needs (a plumber, an emergency clinic), start with SEO and local search instead, or combine the two.

How we work

  1. Audit: we review your page, competitors and what your customers ask before they buy.
  2. Plan: content pillars, a first-month calendar and the enquiry route (WhatsApp, call or form).
  3. Create and publish: you approve the calendar; we design, edit and post.
  4. Reply and report: comments and messages handled, with a monthly report on enquiries, not just reach.

Frequently asked questions

How many posts do I need each month?

Enough to stay consistent with content good enough to be shared. For most local businesses that means 8–12 posts a month, including 4–6 Reels. We recommend a mix based on your goal and budget.

Do you create Reels?

Yes: hooks, scripts, shooting guidance and editing for Reels and short-form video.

Will social media alone bring leads?

Organic content builds trust and demand; paid ads make results faster and more predictable. Most clients combine both.

Do you post in Malayalam?

Yes. We write and edit in Malayalam, English or both, depending on who your customers are.

Do you work with influencers?

Yes. We find local creators whose audience matches your customers, agree the brief and reuse the content in ads where the creator agrees.

How much does social media marketing cost in Kerala?

It depends on the number of posts, Reels and platforms. Our digital marketing cost guide explains typical ranges and what drives the price.
