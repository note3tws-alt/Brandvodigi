How Much Does a Website Cost in Kerala? What Changes the Price

Brandvo Digital Co. · 7 min read · Updated 17 Nov 2026

Ask five agencies about website cost in Kerala and you may get five very different quotes, from a few thousand rupees to several lakhs. That is not always overcharging or undercharging. They are often quoting for very different things. This guide explains what changes the price, so you can compare quotes properly and buy the website your business actually needs.

Start with the job, not the price

Before you ask for quotes, decide what the website must do:

  • Be a digital visiting card: show who you are and how to contact you.
  • Bring enquiries: rank on Google and convert visitors from ads.
  • Sell online: take orders and payments.

A clinic that wants appointment enquiries needs a different site from a jewellery shop that wants to sell online. Most overspending and underspending comes from skipping this step.

The seven things that decide the price

1. Number of pages

A one-page site costs less than a 20-page site with separate pages for each service and location. If you want to rank for several services and towns, you need more pages.

2. Template or custom design

A customised template is quicker and cheaper. A custom design built around your brand takes longer and costs more, but helps you stand out.

3. Content and copywriting

Someone has to write the words and provide the photos. If the agency writes SEO-focused copy and shoots photos, the price rises, but so does the quality. Sites with copied or thin text rarely rank.

4. Features

Booking systems, payment gateways, multilingual content, product catalogues, calculators and integrations with your CRM or billing software each add time.

5. SEO and tracking setup

Proper titles, schema, sitemap, Search Console, Google Analytics and Meta Pixel setup are essential if you want leads. Cheap quotes often leave these out.

6. Speed and quality of build

Optimised images and clean code take more effort than a heavy page builder site, but they load faster on mobile data, which affects both rankings and enquiries.

7. Hosting, domain and maintenance

These are yearly or monthly costs: domain renewal, hosting, security updates, backups and small edits. Ask who pays for them and who owns the accounts.

Typical tiers

Prices vary by agency, so treat these as types of project rather than a price list:

Type What you usually get Good for
Basic landing page One page, contact form, WhatsApp button Testing an offer, running ads
Business website 5–10 pages, SEO setup, tracking, mobile-first Most local service businesses
SEO-led website 15+ pages with service and location pages, written content, schema Businesses competing across several towns
E-commerce store Product catalogue, cart, payments, shipping Retail brands selling online

Questions to ask every agency

  1. Who owns the domain, hosting and website files?
  2. Is copywriting included, or do I provide all content?
  3. Is SEO setup (titles, schema, sitemap, Search Console) included?
  4. Will the site be tested on mobile and for speed?
  5. What does yearly maintenance cost, and what does it cover?
  6. Can I edit text and photos myself?
  7. Can you show live sites you built that rank or bring enquiries?

Red flags

  • The domain is registered in the agency's name
  • No mention of mobile speed or SEO
  • "Unlimited pages" for a very low fixed price
  • Copied text from other websites
  • No contract or written scope

Frequently asked questions

Is a cheap website a bad idea?

Not always. A simple landing page can be enough to start. The risk is paying for a cheap site that cannot rank, loads slowly or cannot be edited, and then paying again to rebuild it.

How long does a business website take to build?

Usually three to six weeks, depending on pages, content and feedback rounds. Landing pages can be faster.

Do I need to pay every year?

Domain and hosting renew yearly. Maintenance is optional but recommended for security and updates.

Will a new website bring customers automatically?

No. A website is the base. Customers come through SEO, Google Business Profile, social media and ads that lead people to it.
