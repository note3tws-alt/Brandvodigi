How Kerala Businesses Can Get Found in Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT

Brandvo Digital Co. · 7 min read · Updated 17 Nov 2026

More customers now ask Google, ChatGPT or Perplexity questions like "Which is the best salon in Kozhikode?" and get one written answer instead of ten links. If your business is not in that answer, you may never be considered. The good news: the work that helps you optimise for Google AI search is mostly the same work that builds a trustworthy local business online. This guide explains what matters and what to do first.

How AI answers choose businesses

AI assistants build answers from sources they can read and trust:

  • Google Business Profile data: ratings, review counts, categories and location. Google's AI Overviews often quote these directly for local searches.
  • Your website: if its text is readable without JavaScript and clearly states who you are, where you are and what you do.
  • Third-party mentions: directories, news, listicles, social profiles and review sites.
  • Consistency: when every source agrees on your name, address, phone and services, the AI is more confident mentioning you.

When sources disagree, AI tools can mix you up with another business, show an old phone number or name the wrong founder. We have seen this happen to local businesses in Kerala, including our own early on.

Seven steps that work

1. Fix your facts everywhere

Make your business name, address, phone, email, website and founders identical on Google, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Justdial and directories. Delete or merge duplicate pages and profiles.

2. Strengthen your Google Business Profile

Reviews and ratings feed directly into local AI answers. Follow our Google Business Profile guide and build a steady review routine.

3. Make your website readable by machines

Many modern websites show text only after JavaScript runs. Some AI crawlers do not run JavaScript, so they see an empty page. Make sure your key pages deliver their text in the HTML itself, and allow AI crawlers in your robots.txt file.

4. Add structured data (schema)

Schema tells search engines exactly what your business is: LocalBusiness or ProfessionalService details, services, FAQs, articles and breadcrumbs. It removes guesswork.

5. Answer real questions on your site

AI tools quote clear answers. Add FAQ sections and guides that answer what customers ask: prices, timelines, areas served, how to choose. Write direct answers in the first sentence, then explain.

6. Create pages for each service and town

If you serve Thalassery, Kasaragod and Kozhikode, have a useful page for each. An AI answering "digital marketing agency in Thalassery" needs a page that clearly says you serve Thalassery. See our Thalassery page as an example.

7. Get mentioned beyond your own site

Local news, industry associations, partner websites, event listings and genuine round-up articles all count as independent confirmation that you exist and do good work.

SEO, AEO and GEO: do you need three strategies?

You will hear terms like AEO (answer engine optimisation) and GEO (generative engine optimisation). For a local business they mostly mean the same thing as good SEO: accurate facts, helpful content, clean technical setup and a strong reputation. You do not need three agencies. You need one plan done well.

How to check where you stand

Ask Google, ChatGPT and Perplexity questions your customers would ask, such as "best [your service] in [your town]" and "[your business name] phone number". Note whether you appear and whether the details are correct. Repeat monthly.

Frequently asked questions

Can I pay to appear in Google AI Overviews?

No. AI Overviews are built from organic sources. Ads may appear near them, but you cannot buy a place inside the answer.

Why does ChatGPT show wrong information about my business?

Usually because old or conflicting information exists online, such as an outdated directory listing, a duplicate Facebook page or an old blog. Fix the sources and the answers improve over time.

Does schema guarantee AI visibility?

No. Schema helps machines understand your site, but reputation, content and consistency matter just as much.

Should I block AI crawlers?

For most local businesses, no. If AI tools cannot read your site, they cannot recommend you.
