Google Business Profile Optimisation for Kerala Businesses: A 12-Step Guide

Brandvo Digital Co. · 8 min read · Updated 17 Nov 2026

If you run a shop, clinic, restaurant or office in Kerala, Google Business Profile optimisation is probably the cheapest way to get more calls this month. When someone searches "bakery near me" or "dentist in Kannur", Google shows a map with three businesses at the top. Those three get most of the calls, direction requests and visits. This guide shows you, step by step, how to give your business a real chance of being one of them.

How Google decides who appears in the map results

Google uses three main signals for local results:

  • Relevance: how well your profile matches what the person searched for.
  • Distance: how close you are to the searcher or the place they named.
  • Prominence: how well known and trusted you are, judged by reviews, links, mentions and activity.

You cannot change your distance. You can improve relevance and prominence, and most of your competitors are not doing it properly.

The 12-step checklist

1. Claim and verify your profile

Search your business name on Google Maps. If a listing exists, claim it; if not, create one at business.google.com. Verification may be by video, phone or postcard. Do not skip it, because unverified profiles rank poorly and can be edited by anyone.

2. Use your real business name

Use the name on your signboard. Adding keywords like "Best Bakery Kannur" to your name breaks Google's rules and can get your profile suspended.

3. Choose the right primary category

This is one of the strongest ranking factors. Pick the most specific category that describes your main business, such as "Dental clinic" rather than "Doctor". Add a few relevant secondary categories.

4. Get your address and service area right

Use the exact address customers visit, with the correct pin code. If you visit customers instead (for example, a plumber or interior designer), set a service area and hide the address.

5. Keep your name, address and phone consistent everywhere

Your details on Google should match your website, Instagram, Facebook, Justdial, Sulekha and any directory exactly. Mismatched phone numbers confuse both Google and customers.

6. Add services and products with descriptions

List each service or product with a short description and, where it makes sense, a price. These fields help Google match you to more searches.

7. Write a helpful business description

Explain what you do, who you serve and where, in plain language. Mention the towns you serve naturally, such as Payyanur, Taliparamba or Kasaragod, without stuffing keywords.

8. Upload real photos regularly

Add your storefront (so people recognise it), interior, team, products and work. Businesses with recent, real photos tend to get more clicks than those with one blurry logo.

9. Build a steady review routine

Ask every satisfied customer for a review. Make it easy with a QR code at the counter and a short WhatsApp message with your review link after a purchase. Steady reviews over months matter more than a burst in one week. Never buy fake reviews; Google removes them and can penalise your profile.

10. Reply to every review

Thank people for positive reviews and respond calmly to negative ones with a solution. Future customers read your replies as closely as the reviews.

11. Post updates and offers

Use Google posts for offers, new products, festival hours and events. Posts keep your profile active and give searchers a reason to choose you.

12. Answer questions and enable messaging

Add common questions and answers yourself (parking, home delivery, payment methods) so customers find answers without calling. Keep opening hours accurate, especially during Onam, Ramadan and other holidays.

Mistakes we see often in Kerala

  • Two or three duplicate listings for the same business, splitting reviews
  • An old phone number nobody answers
  • The wrong pin on the map
  • Keyword-stuffed business names
  • No photos since the shop opened

What to measure

Your profile's Performance section shows searches, calls, website clicks and direction requests. Check it monthly. If calls and direction requests are rising, your profile is working.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to rank on Google Maps?

A complete, verified profile can start appearing within a few weeks. Moving into the top three for competitive searches usually takes several months of reviews, photos and activity.

Can I rank in a town where I do not have an office?

Map results depend heavily on distance, so it is hard. Location pages on your website and service-area settings can help you appear in organic results for nearby towns.

Should I pay someone to get reviews?

No. Buying reviews breaks Google's policies and can get reviews removed or your profile suspended. Ask real customers instead.

Is Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Creating and managing a profile is free.
