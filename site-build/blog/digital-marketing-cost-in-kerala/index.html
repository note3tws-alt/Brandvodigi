Digital Marketing Cost in Kerala: How to Budget for Leads, Not Likes

Brandvo Digital Co. · 8 min read · Updated 17 Nov 2026

"How much does digital marketing cost?" is the first question most business owners ask us, and the honest answer is that digital marketing cost in Kerala depends less on the agency and more on your goal. A bakery that wants more walk-ins and a builder that wants plot buyers need very different budgets. This guide shows you how to think about the money so you pay for leads and sales, not likes.

Two separate costs: the agency fee and the ad spend

Every digital marketing budget has two parts. Keep them separate in every quote you compare.

  • Agency or management fee: what you pay for strategy, content, design, ad management and reporting.
  • Ad spend: what you pay Meta or Google directly to show your ads.

A quote that mixes them hides how much of your money actually reaches customers. Ask for ad spend to be paid from your own ad account, so you can see every rupee.

What changes the agency fee

Scope of work

Managing Instagram and Facebook with 12 posts a month costs less than a full package with Reels shoots, ads, landing pages and SEO.

Content production

Designed posts cost less than shot-and-edited Reels. Video takes more people and time, but it usually performs better.

Number of channels

Meta Ads only, or Meta plus Google Ads, SEO and a website? Each channel adds work.

Reporting and strategy depth

A simple monthly report is cheaper than weekly optimisation with lead tracking and sales reconciliation, but the second one is what improves results.

What decides the right ad spend

Work backwards from what a customer is worth:

  1. Customer value: what is one new customer worth to you over a year?
  2. Acceptable cost per lead: how much can you spend to get one enquiry, given how many enquiries turn into customers?
  3. Lead target: how many enquiries per month can your team actually handle?

Ad spend ≈ acceptable cost per lead × leads you want per month.

A worked example: if a dental implant patient is worth a lot to a clinic and one in five enquiries books, the clinic can afford a much higher cost per lead than a café, where each order is small. The clinic's budget may be larger, but so is the return.

An illustrative way to split a budget

These are example splits for thinking, not fixed rules. The right mix depends on your market.

Business stage Content and management Ad spend SEO and website
Just starting online Most of the budget Small test budget Google Business Profile setup
Steady local business About half About a third Monthly local SEO
Ready to scale About a third About half or more Ongoing SEO and landing pages

Judge results by cost per lead and sales

Likes and followers are cheap. Enquiries and sales are what pay for marketing. Every month, look at:

  • Total enquiries (calls, WhatsApp chats, form leads)
  • Cost per enquiry
  • How many enquiries became customers
  • Revenue from those customers

If an agency's report shows only reach and likes, ask for these numbers. See what we track in performance marketing.

Where Kerala businesses waste money

  • Boosting posts without a target audience or goal
  • Running ads with no way to track calls or WhatsApp chats
  • Changing agencies every two months before anything can be learned
  • Paying for "followers" packages
  • Spending on Google Ads for searches nobody in their town makes

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum budget for digital marketing in Kerala?

There is no fixed minimum. A small business can start with Google Business Profile optimisation and organic content, then add a small ad test. What matters is tracking results so you can scale what works.

Should I pay ad spend to the agency or directly to Meta?

Directly to Meta or Google through your own ad account. You keep control and can see exactly what was spent.

How long before digital marketing pays off?

Ads can bring enquiries within weeks. SEO and brand building take months but keep paying off. Most businesses benefit from both.

Are monthly retainers or one-time projects better?

Websites and branding are usually projects. Social media, ads and SEO need ongoing work, so they are usually monthly.
