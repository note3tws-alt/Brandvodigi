Digital marketing · Wayanad

Digital Marketing for Wayanad Resorts, Homestays and Local Brands

Brandvo Digital Co. is a digital marketing agency for Wayanad businesses. We help resorts, homestays, tour operators, plantation and spice brands, restaurants and local services in Kalpetta, Sulthan Bathery, Mananthavady, Vythiri and Meppadi get more direct bookings and enquiries.

Wayanad businesses depend on people who are not in Wayanad

Most of your customers for a resort, homestay or spice brand live in Bengaluru, Kochi, Kozhikode, Mysuru or the Gulf. They find you on Instagram, YouTube and Google, compare photos and reviews, and book through whoever makes it easiest. If that is an online travel agency, you pay a commission on every booking.

Strong direct marketing reduces that dependence. Every booking that comes through your own Instagram, website or WhatsApp keeps more money with you.

What we do for Wayanad businesses

Resorts and homestays

  • Reels and photo shoots that show the real experience: mist, food, rooms, activities
  • Booking campaigns aimed at travellers from Bengaluru, Kerala cities and the Gulf, timed to long weekends, holidays and the monsoon season
  • A fast website or landing page with direct booking and WhatsApp
  • Google Business Profile optimisation and a review routine

Coffee, tea, spice and plantation brands

Brand identity, packaging design and social content that tells the farm story, plus online sales funnels for retail and gifting. See branding.

Local businesses

Shops, clinics, restaurants and institutes in Kalpetta and Bathery need local visibility. We use Google Maps optimisation and small, tightly targeted ad campaigns.

How we plan a tourism campaign

  1. Audience: which cities and countries your best guests come from
  2. Season: when they plan and when they travel
  3. Content: what makes your property different, shown in the first two seconds
  4. Booking path: direct booking or WhatsApp, with tracking
  5. Review loop: happy guests asked for Google reviews before they leave

Where Brandvo Digital Co. fits

We are a North Kerala agency from Payyanur, a few hours from Wayanad, so we can visit for shoots. We measure success by direct bookings and enquiries, and you own your accounts and content. Book a free strategy call.

Frequently asked questions

Can you reduce my dependence on booking platforms?

Direct marketing can shift part of your bookings to your own channels. We set up direct booking paths and campaigns and track how many bookings come direct.

Do you shoot content on location?

Yes. We plan shoots at your property, timed for the best light and weather.

Which travellers should a Wayanad resort target?

Usually Bengaluru, Kochi, Kozhikode, Mysuru and Kerala natives in the Gulf, but we confirm with your booking data first.

Do you build websites with booking?

Yes. We build fast websites with booking or enquiry forms and WhatsApp. See website design.
