About

About Brandvo Digital Co.

Brandvo Digital Co. is a performance-driven digital marketing and creative agency based in Payyanur, Kannur district, Kerala. We help businesses across Kerala, and Malayali-run businesses in the Gulf, get seen, chosen and remembered, and we measure our work in enquiries, bookings and sales.

Who we are

Brandvo Digital Co. was founded by Muhammed Ansar, with Akshay and Abhishek Prakash as co-founders. The team plans strategy, writes and designs content, creates Reels, runs ads and builds websites, so the brand, the content and the campaigns all come from the same place.

We are based in Payyanur and work with clients across Kerala in person and online.

What we believe

  • Results over vanity metrics. Followers and likes are nice; enquiries and sales pay the bills. Every plan starts with the business number it is meant to move.
  • Plain answers. If a channel won't work for your business, we will say so, even if it means a smaller project for us.
  • Local understanding. Kerala buyers decide differently: WhatsApp is the sales desk, the festival calendar shapes demand, and families in the Gulf often have a say. We plan around that.
  • You own everything. Ad accounts, pages, data and creative stay in your name.

How we work

  1. Discover: understand your business, customers and market.
  2. Strategise: agree the goal, the channels and the budget.
  3. Create: content, campaigns and creative assets.
  4. Launch: campaigns go live on the right platforms.
  5. Optimise: track what brings enquiries, improve every week, report every month.
