Digital marketing · Vadakara

Digital Marketing Agency for Vadakara and North Kozhikode

Brandvo Digital Co. is a digital marketing agency for Vadakara and the northern Kozhikode belt, including Nadapuram, Kuttiady, Kallachi, Perambra and Payyoli. From our base in Payyanur we help retailers, jewellers, clinics, builders and institutes in this region reach local shoppers and the large Gulf-connected community that drives so much of the area's spending.

The Gulf factor changes how you should market

Vadakara and Nadapuram have some of the strongest Gulf links in Kerala. That shapes buying in two ways:

  1. Peak seasons follow the Gulf calendar. Families shop for weddings, homes and gold when relatives come home on leave, around Eid, school holidays and the end of the year.
  2. Decisions are often made from abroad. A son in Dubai or Doha may choose the plot, approve the furniture or pay for the jewellery.

If your marketing only reaches people inside Vadakara, you miss half the decision. If it only runs in festival week, you are late.

What we do for Vadakara businesses

Two-audience campaigns

We run one campaign for local customers and another for people from your area living in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman and Kuwait. Both lead to WhatsApp, which works across time zones.

Content that builds trust

High-value purchases need trust. We create showroom walkthroughs, owner introduction videos, customer stories and clear price or offer posts in Malayalam.

Google Maps for local searches

We optimise your Google Business Profile so you appear when someone searches "jewellery shop Vadakara" or "dentist near Nadapuram", and set up a simple review routine.

Landing pages that convert

For builders, institutes and clinics we create fast landing pages with WhatsApp and call buttons, so ad clicks turn into conversations. See website design.

Frequently asked questions

Can you target Vadakara people living in the Gulf?

Yes. Meta Ads lets us target by country and language, and we can use interest and lookalike audiences built from your existing customers.

When should I start my wedding-season campaign?

Ideally four to six weeks before the season, so your content and ads are already familiar when families start shopping.
