Digital marketing · Malappuram

Digital Marketing Agency in Malappuram for Local and Gulf Customers

Brandvo Digital Co. is a digital marketing agency for Malappuram district businesses. We help textile and wedding stores, jewellers, hospitals, Ayurveda centres, builders, restaurants and institutes in Manjeri, Perinthalmanna, Tirur, Kottakkal, Kondotty, Nilambur and Ponnani grow through social media, paid ads and Google.

Two things make Malappuram different

A young, mobile-first audience. Malappuram is one of Kerala's most populous districts with a large young population that discovers shops, food and fashion on Instagram and YouTube first.

Strong Gulf links. Many families have members working in the GCC. They shape spending on homes, weddings, gold and vehicles, often deciding from abroad.

Marketing that ignores either group leaves money on the table. Marketing that speaks to both, with content locals share and campaigns that reach Malappuram natives in the Gulf, can grow quickly.

What we do for Malappuram businesses

Wedding, textile and jewellery campaigns

Bridal collections, groom wear, festival and Eid campaigns, planned weeks ahead of each season and run to local families and Gulf-based relatives.

Healthcare and Ayurveda

Perinthalmanna and Kottakkal draw patients from across North Kerala and beyond. We create trust-building content and booking flows within healthcare ad policies. See healthcare marketing.

Restaurants and food brands

Reels that make people hungry, launch campaigns for new outlets and local targeting that brings walk-ins and orders.

Real estate and construction

Lead campaigns for plots, villas and apartments, with separate targeting for NRI buyers. See real estate marketing.

Google Maps visibility

We optimise your Google Business Profile for searches like "textile shop Manjeri" or "hospital near Tirur" and set up a review routine.

Frequently asked questions

Can you run ads to Malappuram people living in the Gulf?

Yes. We can target by country and language and run separate campaigns for the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman and Kuwait.

Which towns in Malappuram do you cover?

All of the district, including Manjeri, Perinthalmanna, Tirur, Kottakkal, Kondotty, Nilambur, Ponnani and Malappuram town.

Do you make Reels?

Yes. Short-form video is central to most of our social media work, from scripting and shooting to editing and posting.

How soon will I get enquiries?

Ads usually start bringing enquiries in the first one to two weeks once tracking is set up. SEO and Maps rankings take a few months.
