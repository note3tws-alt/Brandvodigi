Why Payyanur businesses need a different plan

Payyanur is a town where people still ask friends first, then check Instagram, then message on WhatsApp. A good plan follows that path instead of copying what works in Kochi or Bengaluru.

  • Word of mouth moves online. Customers look up the shop or builder they heard about. If your Instagram looks inactive or your Google listing has no reviews, they move on.
  • WhatsApp is the sales desk. Most enquiries here end in a WhatsApp chat or a call, not a website form. Campaigns should send people straight there and track it.
  • The audience is small, so waste shows fast. Ads shown to all of Kannur district burn budget on people who will never drive to Payyanur. Tight radius targeting matters.
  • Gulf-connected families decide together. For property, weddings and big purchases, the buyer may be in the UAE and the family here. Content has to work for both.
  • Malayalam converts. English builds credibility; Malayalam captions and voiceovers usually get the response.
