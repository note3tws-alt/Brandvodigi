Healthcare and Dental Clinic Marketing That Builds Trust First

Brandvo Digital Co. is a healthcare digital marketing agency in Kerala for hospitals, dental clinics, specialist clinics, physiotherapy, Ayurveda and wellness centres. We help patients find you, trust you and book an appointment, while keeping your marketing within advertising rules and medical ethics.

Patients choose a doctor the way they choose family

People do not pick a clinic the way they pick a café. They ask relatives, read Google reviews, look at the doctor's background and check how easy it is to get an appointment. Clinics that look outdated online, or that have a few unanswered negative reviews, lose patients they never knew about.

At the same time, healthcare advertising has strict limits. Meta restricts targeting around health conditions and rejects ads that make assumptions about personal health. Exaggerated promises damage trust and can get your account restricted.

What we do for healthcare providers

Google Business Profile and reviews

Correct categories, services, timings and photos, plus a polite review request routine after appointments. For most clinics, this is the biggest source of new patients. See our Google Business Profile guide.

Doctor-led content

Short Malayalam videos where your doctors explain common conditions, procedures and prevention. Patients meet the doctor before they visit, which builds trust no ad can buy.

Policy-safe Meta Ads

Ads that promote your services, facilities and expertise without implying anything about the viewer's health. Targeting by location and broad interests, never by sensitive conditions.

Appointment booking

WhatsApp booking, call buttons and simple forms, with tracking so you know how many appointments each channel brings.

Website and service pages

A fast website with a page for each department or treatment, doctor profiles and clear contact details. See website design.

What we will not do

  • Promise cures or guaranteed results
  • Use misleading before-and-after images
  • Share or upload patient data without consent
  • Buy fake reviews

Where Brandvo Digital Co. fits

We plan healthcare marketing around trust and access, and we measure appointments and enquiries rather than likes. We work with clinics across Kerala, from Kannur to Trivandrum. Talk to us about your clinic.

Frequently asked questions

Can clinics run Facebook and Instagram ads?

Yes, within Meta's policies. Ads can promote services and facilities but must not target or imply personal health conditions.

What works best for dental clinics?

A strong Google Business Profile with steady reviews, doctor videos explaining treatments, and local ads for services like aligners, implants or check-ups, with easy WhatsApp booking.

Do you write content in Malayalam?

Yes. Most patient education content works best in simple Malayalam.

How do you measure results for a clinic?

Calls, WhatsApp chats, booking form submissions and, where your front desk records it, how many became appointments.
