Real Estate Leads From People Who Actually Want to Buy

Brandvo Digital Co. is a real estate digital marketing agency in Kerala helping builders, developers, brokers and land sellers get enquiries from serious buyers instead of time-wasters. Our clients include SAJ Realty and Land4Sale Payyanur.

The real estate lead problem

Cheap leads are easy. Most of them never pick up the phone. Property is a high-value family decision, often made with relatives in the Gulf, and it can take months. A campaign that only counts form fills will fill your sales team's day with people who were just curious.

What a builder or broker actually needs is fewer, better enquiries: people with a budget, a location in mind and a timeline, who answer when you call back.

How Kerala property buyers decide

  • They research for weeks. Instagram, Facebook groups, YouTube walkthroughs and Google Maps come long before a site visit.
  • Family and NRI relatives have a say. A plot in Kannur or a flat in Kochi is often funded or approved by someone in Dubai, Doha or Riyadh.
  • Trust comes first. Approvals, title clarity, road access, the builder's past projects and how quickly the sales team replies all matter more than a slick brochure.
  • WhatsApp is the sales desk. Most first conversations happen there, not on a phone call or email.

How we do it

Qualifying ads and forms

Lead forms with questions on budget, preferred location, property type and timeline. Higher-intent forms produce fewer leads, but far more of them turn into site visits.

Creative that sells the plot or home

Walkthrough Reels, drone or phone footage of the location, distance to schools, hospitals and highways, and clear price ranges, in Malayalam and English.

NRI-aware targeting

Campaigns that reach Gulf-based Malayalis and their families in Kerala, timed for Gulf weekends and the holiday season when NRIs visit home.

Fast follow-up

WhatsApp flows and a shared lead sheet so every enquiry gets a reply quickly, plus reminders so warm leads are not forgotten after the first call.

Local search and listings

A Google Business Profile for each office or project, reviews from real buyers, and project pages that show up when people search the area. See SEO and local search.

Reporting that sales teams trust

We report on qualified leads, site visits and bookings you share with us, not just form fills and clicks.

Who we work with

Builders and developers launching villa or apartment projects, land and plot sellers, brokers and property consultants, and interior and home businesses that sell to new home owners. We are based in Payyanur and work across Kannur, Kozhikode, Kochi and the rest of Kerala.

A note on compliance

Projects that must be registered with K-RERA need their registration details in advertising. We build that into the creative and landing pages from the start, and we check Meta's current advertising policies for property ads before every launch.

Frequently asked questions

Can Meta Ads generate real estate leads in Kerala?

Yes, when targeting, creative and qualification questions are set up for buyers, and leads are followed up quickly.

Do you target NRI buyers?

Yes. We can target Malayali audiences in the UAE and other Gulf countries alongside families in Kerala.

Why are my real estate leads low quality?

Usually because the form is too easy, the ad promises too much, or nobody calls back fast enough. Adding qualifying questions and a same-day follow-up routine fixes most of it.

How much should a builder spend on ads?

It depends on the project value, location and how many units are left. We start with a test budget, measure cost per qualified lead, then scale what works.

Do you make walkthrough videos?

We script and edit walkthrough Reels and can guide your team or a local videographer on the shoot.
