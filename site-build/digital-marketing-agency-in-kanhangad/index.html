Digital marketing · Kanhangad

Digital Marketing for Kanhangad and Nileshwar Businesses

Brandvo Digital Co. is a digital marketing agency for Kanhangad businesses, based just down the coast in Payyanur. Kanhangad is the biggest commercial town in Kasaragod district, and we help its retailers, clinics, builders, schools and hospitality businesses, along with neighbours in Nileshwar, Cheruvathur and Trikaripur, get found online and turn that attention into enquiries.

Why Kanhangad businesses need a sharper online presence

Kanhangad draws shoppers from a wide area along the coast and the hill roads. Tourists heading to Bekal Fort and the beaches pass through it. Yet when people search for a textile shop, a dentist or a builder in Kanhangad, the results are often thin: outdated Google listings, Facebook pages with no posts this year, and directory pages with wrong phone numbers.

That is good news for you. A business that shows up properly here, with an updated Google profile, regular Reels and simple ads, can stand out quickly because competition is lighter than in Kannur or Kozhikode.

A practical starting plan for Kanhangad

Weeks 1–2: get the basics right

  • Claim and complete your Google Business Profile with categories, photos, hours and services.
  • Make your name, address and phone identical on Google, Instagram, Facebook, Justdial and your website.
  • Add WhatsApp click-to-chat everywhere a customer can contact you.

Weeks 3–4: start content and ads

  • Post three to four Reels a week showing products, team and customer moments.
  • Launch a small Meta Ads campaign aimed at Kanhangad, Nileshwar and nearby areas.
  • Track every call, chat and form so you know the cost of each enquiry.

Month 2 onward: scale what works

  • Shift budget to the ads and content that bring enquiries.
  • Ask every happy customer for a Google review.
  • Plan festival campaigns for Onam, Vishu, Eid and Christmas well in advance.
