## Kannur district is several markets, not one

A campaign that works in Kannur town can fail in Iritty or Payyanur. Before we spend a rupee, we decide which towns, which buyers and which radius matter for your business.

- **Kannur town (Thana, Fort Road, Caltex, South Bazaar):** the district's busiest retail and services market. Shoppers compare on Instagram and Google before they visit, so reviews, photos and a clear offer decide who gets the call.
- **Thalassery and Kuthuparamba:** strong food, bakery and family-business culture. Reputation and repeat customers drive growth; content that shows people and craft works better than discount posts. See our [Thalassery page](/digital-marketing-agency-in-thalassery/).
- **Taliparamba:** a trading and education hub on the national highway. Retailers, coaching centres and clinics here draw customers from surrounding villages, so radius targeting has to be wider than you might think. See our [Taliparamba page](/digital-marketing-agency-in-taliparamba/).
- **Payyanur and Pilathara:** close-knit towns where trust and WhatsApp conversations close the sale. This is our home market. See our [Payyanur page](/digital-marketing-agency-in-payyanur/).
- **Mattannur:** the Kannur International Airport has brought hotels, travel, real estate and new retail to the area. Many buyers here are first-time visitors or Gulf travellers, so Google Maps visibility matters more than social reach.
- **Iritty and the hill towns:** plantation, trading and hill-route businesses towards the Karnataka border. Smaller audiences mean every rupee of ad spend has to stay inside the right radius.

Seasons matter across the district. Onam, Vishu, Eid, Christmas, the wedding season and the Theyyam season (roughly October to May in North Malabar) change what people search for, where they travel and what they buy. We plan content and budgets around them instead of posting the same thing every week.

## What usually holds Kannur businesses back online

These are the patterns we see most often when a Kannur business asks us to look at their marketing:

1. **No Google Business Profile, or one with five reviews and no photos.** For "near me" and "in Kannur" searches, the map listings get most of the attention. A good profile and a steady review routine is often the cheapest win available. Our [Google Business Profile guide](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-kerala/) explains how.
2. **Boosting posts instead of running campaigns.** Boosts are optimised for likes and reach, not enquiries. A proper Meta Ads campaign with a lead form or WhatsApp button, tracked properly, costs the same and tells you what each enquiry cost.
3. **Ads shown to the whole district.** A salon in Thalassery does not need customers in Cherupuzha. Tight radius targeting by town cuts waste quickly.
4. **English-only content.** English builds credibility, but Malayalam captions and voiceovers usually get the response. Most of our Kannur campaigns run in both.
5. **No way to tell which enquiries came from marketing.** If calls and WhatsApp chats are not tracked, nobody knows what is working. We set up tracking before we scale spend.
