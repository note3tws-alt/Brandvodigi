## Kozhikode is crowded online. Generic marketing disappears.

Calicut has a strong food culture, a busy fashion and beauty scene, and plenty of agencies. Every category has dozens of businesses posting every day. In a market like this, "posting regularly" is not a strategy. What stands out is a clear brand, content people actually stop for, and ads aimed at the customers worth having.

Businesses that blend in pay more for each customer. Businesses with a distinct brand and a clear offer pay less, because their content and ads earn attention instead of buying it.
