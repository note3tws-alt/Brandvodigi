We design logos, brand identities, packaging, signage, posters and social media templates, and we connect them to marketing that brings customers.

## Why branding is a sales tool, not decoration

People judge a business in seconds. Before they read a word, your logo, colours, photos and posts tell them whether you are professional, affordable, premium or risky. A strong brand lets you charge fairly, makes ads cheaper because people recognise and trust you, and turns first-time buyers into repeat customers.

A weak or inconsistent brand does the opposite. Different logos on your board, Instagram and WhatsApp, random colours and stock photos all make customers hesitate, and hesitation costs sales.

## What our branding service covers

### Rebrands
For established businesses that have outgrown their look, we refresh the brand while keeping the recognition you have built.

## Our process

1. **Discovery workshop:** your story, customers, competitors and goals.
2. **Strategy and direction:** positioning and two or three visual directions to choose from.
3. **Design and refinement:** we develop the chosen direction with your feedback.
4. **Brand kit and handover:** all files, a simple brand guide and templates for daily use.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a branding project take?
A logo and core identity usually takes two to four weeks. A full brand system with packaging and signage can take longer, depending on scope.

### Do I own the logo and files?
Yes. You receive all final files and full rights once the project is paid.

### Can you redesign my logo without losing recognition?
Yes. A refresh can modernise your logo while keeping the colours, shapes or name style customers already know.

### Do you also handle printing?
We prepare print-ready files and can coordinate with printers you prefer or recommend local options.
