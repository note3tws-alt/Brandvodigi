## A growing town with a wide catchment

Taliparamba is a commercial centre for a large rural catchment: people come in from Alakode, Sreekandapuram, Pariyaram, Kuppam and the hill areas to shop, study and see doctors. Pilgrims visit the Rajarajeshwara Temple and nearby Parassinikadavu. That catchment is the opportunity. Your next customer may live 25 km away and decide which shop to visit after scrolling Instagram the night before.

The problem most Taliparamba businesses face is not quality. It is visibility outside the town centre. Ads and content that reach only your existing followers will not bring customers from the surrounding villages.

## Frequently asked questions

### How quickly can I see enquiries from ads?
Paid campaigns usually start bringing enquiries within the first one to two weeks, once tracking is in place. SEO and Google Maps rankings take longer, usually a few months of consistent work.

### Can you target people outside Taliparamba town?
Yes. We can target a radius around your location or specific nearby towns and panchayats, and we exclude areas that never convert.

### Do I need a website, or is Instagram enough?
Instagram can be enough to start. A simple, fast website or landing page helps when you run ads, want Google rankings or sell higher-value products. Read [what a website costs in Kerala](/blog/website-cost-in-kerala/).
