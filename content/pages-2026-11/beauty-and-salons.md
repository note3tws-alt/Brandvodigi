## Beauty is bought with the eyes

Your clients decide on Instagram. They look at your transformations, your interiors, your team and how other clients look after visiting you. A feed full of random templates and stock photos says "average salon" even when your work is excellent. A feed with real, well-shot transformations and a consistent look says "worth the price".

The difference shows up in your booking calendar and in what you can charge.
