## Most small business websites lose customers quietly

A slow site, a phone number that is hard to find, a menu that does not work on mobile, or a contact form that nobody checks: each one sends a ready customer to a competitor. You never see those losses, so the website looks "fine". The question is not whether you have a website. It is whether your website brings you business.

## What we build

### Business websites
A complete site with your services, locations, work, reviews and contact options, built so Google can read every page. Good for clinics, builders, schools, showrooms and professional services.

### Landing pages for ads
A single focused page for one offer or campaign, with a short form and a WhatsApp button. Landing pages usually convert ad traffic better than a homepage because there is nothing to distract the visitor. See [Meta Ads](/services/meta-ads/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a website take?
A landing page can be ready in about a week once content is final. A full business website usually takes three to six weeks, depending on pages and feedback rounds.

### Do you use WordPress?
We choose the platform that fits your needs: WordPress for sites you want to edit often, or lightweight static builds for maximum speed. We explain the trade-offs before we start.

### Will my website rank on Google?
We build every site with SEO foundations, which is necessary but not enough on its own. Ranking for competitive searches also needs content and local SEO over time.

### Can you redesign my existing site without losing rankings?
Yes. We map old URLs to new ones with redirects and keep the content that ranks, so a redesign does not wipe out your search traffic.
