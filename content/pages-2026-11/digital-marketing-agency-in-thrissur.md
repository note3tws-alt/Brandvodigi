## Thrissur buys on trust, so marketing must earn it

Thrissur is known for its jewellery and textile trade, its festivals and its long-established family businesses. Customers here are careful with big purchases. They compare, ask relatives, check reviews and visit more than one shop. Flashy ads with no substance do not convince them. Proof does: real customers, clear prices or offers, the owner's face and a long track record told well.

A business that only advertises discounts trains customers to wait for the next sale. A business that builds trust online can sell at fair prices all year.
