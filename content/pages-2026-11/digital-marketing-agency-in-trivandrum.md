## A capital city audience with its own habits

Trivandrum's customers include government employees, Technopark professionals, students preparing for competitive exams, and a steady flow of medical and tourism visitors. They research carefully and compare options online. Many decisions, like choosing a hospital, a coaching centre or a school, involve the whole family.

That means generic "brand awareness" posts rarely move the needle. What works is specific: a clear answer to the customer's question, proof that you deliver, and an easy way to ask a question on WhatsApp.
