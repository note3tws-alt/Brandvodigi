**Unicorn Bakes** is a pastry and cake shop in Payyanur, Kannur district. Working with Brandvo Digital Co. on content, influencer marketing, Meta Ads and branding, the business generated **₹95 lakh in sales from ₹8 lakh in ad spend over two years**, roughly ₹11.9 in sales for every ₹1 spent on ads.

## The challenge

A bakery lives on repeat customers and occasions: birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, festivals and office celebrations. Unicorn Bakes needed people across Payyanur and nearby towns to think of them first whenever there was something to celebrate, and to order without hesitation.

Like most local food businesses, they faced three problems:

- Plenty of competition from other bakeries and home bakers on Instagram
- Occasion orders that are decided quickly, often the same day
- Ad spend that had to pay for itself in orders, not likes

## What we did

### A brand people remember
We gave Unicorn Bakes a consistent, appetising look across Instagram, Facebook, packaging and in-store, so every post and every box felt like the same premium brand. See [branding and creative design](/services/branding/).

### Content that makes people hungry
Short videos and photos of cakes, pastries and custom orders, planned around the occasions people celebrate and the Kerala festival calendar. See [social media marketing](/services/social-media-marketing/).

### Influencer marketing
We brought in local food and lifestyle creators whose followers match Unicorn Bakes' customers, so new people heard about the shop from someone they already trust. Creator content also gave us real, relatable material to reuse in ads.

### Meta Ads aimed at orders
Facebook and Instagram campaigns targeted to people around Payyanur and nearby towns, with WhatsApp and calls as the way to order. We kept spending on what brought orders and cut what did not. See [Meta Ads](/services/meta-ads/).

### Tracking that tied ads to sales
We watched orders, not just clicks, so every budget decision was based on what actually sold.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is ROAS?
Return on ad spend: sales divided by ad spend. ₹95 lakh in sales from ₹8 lakh of ad spend over two years is a ROAS of about 11.9.

### Can every business get this result?
No. Results depend on the product, margins, market, budget and how well enquiries are handled. We plan around your numbers and report honestly on what the ads deliver.
