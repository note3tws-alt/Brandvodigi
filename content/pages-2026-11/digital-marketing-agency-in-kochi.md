## Kochi is Kerala's most expensive place to buy attention

Kochi has the most brands, the most agencies and the busiest feeds in the state. Ad auctions here are more competitive than in North Kerala, so the same budget usually buys fewer clicks. Businesses that copy what everyone else posts end up paying more for each enquiry every month.

What works in Kochi is precision: a clear offer, creative that stops the scroll, tight location targeting, and tracking that shows which rupee brought which customer.

## How we market Kochi businesses

### Neighbourhood-level targeting
A café in Panampilly Nagar and a furniture showroom in Edappally have different catchments. We target by area and by commute patterns, around Infopark and Kakkanad, along the Metro line and near the malls, instead of "all of Ernakulam".

### Creative built for a crowded feed
Short Reels with a hook in the first two seconds, real people instead of stock images, and offers that are easy to understand. For entertainment and lifestyle brands like Playbox Club, we focus on the experience: friends, competition and weekend plans.

### Lead and booking funnels
Meta Ads lead forms, WhatsApp click-to-chat and landing pages, picked to suit your sales process, with every enquiry tracked so we can lower the cost of each one over time. See [Meta Ads](/services/meta-ads/) and [performance marketing](/services/performance-marketing/).

### Local SEO for "near me" searches
We optimise your Google Business Profile and pages for the neighbourhoods you serve, so you show up when people search "salon near Kadavanthra" or "dentist in Aluva". See [SEO](/services/seo/).
