## Palakkad sits between two markets

Palakkad is Kerala's gateway to Tamil Nadu. Many customers speak both Malayalam and Tamil, and buyers near the border often compare you with options in Coimbatore. The district also mixes a strong farming economy with industry around Kanjikode.

For marketing, that means:

- **Language matters.** Some audiences respond better to Tamil creative. We test both instead of guessing.
- **You compete with Coimbatore.** Your content needs to show why buying locally is better: service, trust, convenience.
- **B2B and B2C need different playbooks.** A manufacturer looking for dealers needs a very different funnel from a textile shop looking for walk-ins.
