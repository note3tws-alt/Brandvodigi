Brandvo Digital Co. is an **SEO company in Kannur** that helps Kerala businesses get found on Google Search, Google Maps and the new AI answers. We focus on searches that lead to calls, visits and enquiries, like "dentist in Payyanur" or "builders in Kannur", not traffic for its own sake.

## Why SEO matters for local businesses

When someone searches with a place name or "near me", they usually want to buy soon. Those searches are free to win, and they keep bringing customers after the work is done, unlike ads that stop when the budget stops. The catch is that SEO takes time, and most businesses give up before it pays off or hire someone who only sends keyword reports.

## What our SEO service includes

### 1. Google Business Profile optimisation
For most local businesses this is the biggest win. We set the right categories, services, products, photos, opening hours and Q&A, and build a simple review routine. Read our [Google Business Profile guide](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-kerala/).

### 2. Technical SEO
We check that Google can crawl and index your site: speed on mobile, page titles, headings, broken links, sitemaps, structured data (schema) and duplicate pages. We fix what we can directly and give your developer a clear list for the rest.

### 3. Location and service pages
If you serve several towns, one homepage cannot rank for all of them. We plan pages for each important service and location, written for real customers, not stuffed with keywords.

### 4. Content that answers buyers' questions
Guides, FAQs and comparison pages that answer what customers ask before they buy. This also helps you appear in Google's AI Overviews and in ChatGPT answers. See [how to get found in AI search](/blog/google-ai-overviews-for-local-business-kerala/).

### 5. Citations and links
Consistent listings on Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART and industry directories, and local links from associations, partners and news where possible. No paid link schemes.

### 6. Reporting you can understand
Monthly reports from Google Search Console and your Google Business Profile: searches you appear for, clicks, calls, direction requests and what we are doing next.

## How long does SEO take?

Fixes to your Google profile and website can show results within weeks. Ranking for competitive searches like "best digital marketing agency in Kerala" usually takes six to twelve months of steady work. Anyone who guarantees a number one ranking is guessing. We give you a realistic plan and show progress every month.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you guarantee first-page rankings?
No. Nobody controls Google's rankings. We guarantee the work: a clear plan, monthly progress and honest reporting.

### Do I need a new website for SEO?
Not always. Many sites can be fixed. If your site is very slow, cannot be edited or is built in a way Google cannot read, we will recommend a rebuild and explain why.

### Do you do SEO for businesses outside Kannur?
Yes. We work across Kerala and with businesses targeting the UAE.

### What is local SEO?
Local SEO is the work that helps you appear for searches with a place name or "near me", mainly in Google Maps and the local results. It focuses on your Google Business Profile, reviews, listings and location pages.
