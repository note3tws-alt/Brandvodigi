If you search for the **best digital marketing agency in Kerala**, you will find dozens of agencies in Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, Kozhikode, Kannur and beyond, each claiming the top spot. None of them can prove it, because there is no official ranking. What exists is a better question: which agency is the best fit for your business, your budget and your goal?

This checklist gives you nine things to compare. Use it on every agency you speak to, including us.

## Why "best" depends on your business

A bakery in Payyanur, a dental clinic in Kozhikode and a plot developer in Kochi need very different marketing. The bakery needs footfall and WhatsApp orders. The clinic needs appointment bookings from people already searching for treatment. The developer needs qualified site-visit leads. An agency that is excellent at one of these can be average at another.

So instead of asking who is the best, ask who has done work like yours, and can show you how.

## The 9-point checklist

### 1. They start with your business goal, not a package

A good first call sounds like a conversation about your numbers: average order value, how many leads you can handle, what a new customer is worth. If an agency opens with a price list of "Silver, Gold and Platinum" packages before asking about your goal, they are selling a product, not solving a problem.

### 2. Their proof is specific and checkable

"We have grown many brands" proves nothing. Ask for named clients you can contact, live pages or accounts you can look at, and what changed for those clients: more enquiries, lower cost per lead, higher bookings. Be wary of screenshots with no context.

### 3. You know who actually does the work

Ask who will run your ads, who will design your creatives and who you will talk to every week. Some agencies sell with a senior team and hand the work to juniors, or outsource it entirely. There is nothing wrong with a small team, as long as it is clear who is responsible for what.

### 4. They understand Kerala as several markets, not one

Customer behaviour in Kochi, Kozhikode, Kannur and Thiruvananthapuram can differ, and so do festival calendars, languages and price sensitivity. A strong local agency plans around Onam, Vishu, Eid and Christmas, writes in natural Malayalam where it helps, and knows that many families here have a Gulf connection that shapes how they shop and what they trust.

### 5. Reporting is simple and tied to money

Reports should lead with outcomes: leads, cost per lead, bookings, sales and return on ad spend. Reach, impressions and follower counts are context, not results. Ask for a sample report before you sign. If you cannot understand it in five minutes, it was not written for you.

### 6. You own your accounts and assets

Your ad account, Meta pixel, Google Business Profile, website, domain and creative files should all be in your name. If an agency insists on running ads from their own account and keeping the data, leaving them later becomes painful. Ask this question early.

### 7. Pricing is transparent

Agency fees (usually a monthly retainer) and ad spend (paid directly to Meta or Google) are two separate things. A clear quote lists what is included each month: number of creatives, reels, campaigns, reports and calls. Hidden extras are a sign of trouble.

### 8. Creative and performance sit together

Good targeting cannot rescue weak creative, and beautiful creative that is never tested wastes budget. The best results come when the people designing the content see the ad data and adjust. Ask how designers and media buyers work together.

### 9. They make realistic promises

Walk away from anyone who guarantees the first position on Google, a fixed number of sales, or results in a week. Honest agencies talk about testing, ranges and timelines. Paid ads usually produce useful data in the first few weeks and become more efficient over two to three months. SEO typically takes longer.

## Questions to ask on the first call

- What would you want to know about my business before suggesting a plan?
- Can I speak to two current clients in a business similar to mine?
- Who will manage my account day to day?
- What will you measure in month one, and how does it connect to sales?
- Who owns the ad account and the data?
- What happens if we are not seeing progress after 90 days?
- What would you choose not to do for my business, and why?

## What to expect for different industries in Kerala

- **Real estate and land:** lead generation on Meta and Google, site-visit tracking, and fast follow-up on WhatsApp.
- **Clinics and dental practices:** Google Business Profile, local search and trust-building content such as patient education.
- **Salons, boutiques and bakeries:** Instagram and Reels, offers around festivals, and click-to-WhatsApp ads.
- **E-commerce and retail:** product feeds, retargeting and creative testing.
- **Restaurants and hospitality:** local search, reviews and seasonal campaigns.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a digital marketing agency cost in Kerala?

It varies widely with scope. Monthly retainers depend on how many channels, creatives and campaigns are included, and your ad budget is separate. Ask each agency for an itemised quote so you can compare like with like instead of comparing headline prices.

### How long before I see results?

Paid campaigns typically start producing usable data within a few weeks, and performance improves as the agency tests and learns. SEO is a longer game, often several months. Anyone promising overnight results is guessing.

### Should I hire an agency, a freelancer or an in-house marketer?

A freelancer can be a good fit for a single task such as design or ad setup. An in-house marketer suits larger businesses with steady needs. An agency makes sense when you need strategy, creative, paid media and reporting working together without hiring four people.
