Brandvo Digital Co. is a **real estate digital marketing agency in Kerala** helping builders, developers, brokers and land sellers get enquiries from serious buyers instead of time-wasters.

## The real estate lead problem

Cheap leads are easy. Most of them never pick up the phone. Property is a high-value, family decision, often involving relatives in the Gulf, so campaigns have to filter for intent before your sales team spends time.

## How we do it

- **Qualifying ads and forms:** budget, location and timeline questions that filter out casual clicks.
- **Creative that sells the plot or home:** walkthrough Reels, location benefits, in Malayalam and English.
- **NRI-aware targeting:** reaching Gulf-based Malayalis and their families in Kerala.
- **Fast follow-up:** WhatsApp flows and lead sheets so every lead gets a reply quickly.
- **Reporting on qualified leads and site visits,** not just form fills.
