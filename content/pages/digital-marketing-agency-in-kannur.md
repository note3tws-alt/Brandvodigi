## North Malabar is several markets, not one

A campaign that works in Kannur town can fail in Thalassery or Kasaragod. We plan by place:

- **Kannur town and Taliparamba:** busy retail and service markets where shoppers compare on Instagram and Google before visiting.
- **Thalassery:** strong food, bakery and family-business culture; reputation and repeat customers drive growth.
- **Payyanur and Pilathara:** close-knit towns where trust and WhatsApp conversations close the sale.
- **Kasaragod:** bilingual audiences (Malayalam and Kannada) and many Gulf-connected households.

Festivals and seasons matter here too: Onam, Vishu, Eid, Christmas, the wedding season and the Theyyam season change what people search for and buy. We plan content and budgets around them.
