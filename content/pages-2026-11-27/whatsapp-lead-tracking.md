In Kerala, most enquiries arrive on WhatsApp, not through website forms. That is great for conversion, but it creates a blind spot: when a message says "Hi, price?", you have no idea whether it came from your Instagram ad, Google Maps or a friend's recommendation. **WhatsApp lead tracking** fixes that blind spot with a few small habits, so you can see which spend actually brings customers.

## Why this matters

Without tracking, owners judge ads by feel. A campaign that "brought lots of messages" may have brought mostly price-shoppers, while a quieter one brought the customers who paid. When you know the source of every chat, you can answer the only questions that matter:

- How much did each enquiry cost?
- How many enquiries became sales?
- Which channel should get more budget next month?

## Step 1: Give every source its own starting message

Every link to your WhatsApp can carry a pre-filled message. The customer only has to press send, and the first message tells you where they came from.

| Source | Pre-filled message example |
| --- | --- |
| Website button | Hi, I found you on your website |
| Google Business Profile | Hi, I found you on Google |
| Instagram bio | Hi, I saw your Instagram |
| Meta click-to-WhatsApp ad (cake offer) | Hi, I'm interested in the custom cake offer |

A wa.me link with a message looks like this: `https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hi%2C%20I%20found%20you%20on%20Google`. In Meta Ads, click-to-WhatsApp campaigns let you set the greeting and pre-filled message for each ad, so give each campaign its own wording.

## Step 2: Label every chat in WhatsApp Business

WhatsApp Business has labels. Create one label per source (Website, Google, Instagram, Meta Ad, Referral) and one per stage (New, Quoted, Won, Lost). Whoever handles the chat adds both labels on the first reply. It takes three seconds and turns your chat list into a simple sales pipeline.

## Step 3: Keep a one-line log

At the end of each day, add one line per new enquiry to a shared Google Sheet: date, name, source label, what they asked about, and whether it became a sale (and the value). Small businesses don't need a CRM to start; a sheet that is actually filled in beats a CRM that nobody updates.

## Step 4: Track website clicks in Google Analytics

If your website has a WhatsApp button, set up a click event in Google Analytics 4 so you can see which pages and which traffic sources lead to WhatsApp chats. Count it as a key event, but remember a click is not yet a lead: the label and log tell you what happened next.

## Step 5: Do the monthly maths

At the end of the month, compare spend with results for each source:

| Source | Spend | Enquiries | Sales | Cost per enquiry | Cost per sale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Meta Ads | Your spend | From labels | From log | Spend ÷ enquiries | Spend ÷ sales |
| Google Maps | ₹0 | From labels | From log | ₹0 | ₹0 |

The table above is a template; fill it with your own numbers. Often the cheapest enquiries are not the cheapest sales, which is why the last column matters most.

## When to move to the WhatsApp Business API

If you get many enquiries a day or several people answer chats, the WhatsApp Business Platform (API) with a shared inbox or CRM lets you assign chats, record the source automatically and send approved follow-up messages. It costs more and takes setup time, so start with labels and a sheet, and move up when volume demands it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I track WhatsApp leads without paid software?

Yes. Pre-filled messages, WhatsApp Business labels and a shared Google Sheet are free and work well for most small businesses.

### Do Meta Ads show how many WhatsApp chats an ad started?

Yes. Click-to-WhatsApp campaigns report messaging conversations started. They do not tell you which chats became sales, which is why your own labels and log matter.

### What if the customer deletes the pre-filled message?

Some will. Ask "How did you find us?" in your first reply for any chat without a recognisable starting message, and label it from the answer.

### Is a WhatsApp click the same as a lead?

No. A click shows interest. A lead is a person who sends a real enquiry, and a sale is what pays. Track all three separately.
