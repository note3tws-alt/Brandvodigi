A new patient choosing a dentist in Kannur, Kozhikode or Kochi usually does three things: searches Google, looks at the map results, and reads reviews before calling. **Dental clinic marketing in Kerala** is mostly about winning those three moments, with a few careful ads on top. This guide explains what to fix first, what to avoid, and how to measure whether it is working.

## How patients actually find a dentist

Most dental searches are local and urgent or near-urgent: "dentist near me", "root canal in Thalassery", "kids dentist Kozhikode", "dental clinic open Sunday". Patients rarely scroll far. They choose from the map results, check the star rating and recent reviews, look at photos of the clinic, and then call or send a WhatsApp message.

That means your Google Business Profile and reviews often matter more than your Instagram follower count.

## Step 1: Make your Google Business Profile complete

- **Primary category:** "Dental clinic" (or "Dentist" for a solo practice). Add specific secondary categories only if they are true, such as "Orthodontist" or "Pediatric dentist".
- **Services:** list treatments you offer, such as scaling and cleaning, fillings, root canal treatment, crowns, braces and aligners, implants, with a one-line plain description each.
- **Hours:** keep them exact, including Sunday and festival hours. Wrong hours lead to angry reviews.
- **Photos:** reception, treatment room, equipment, sterilisation area and the team. Real photos build trust with anxious patients.
- **Appointment link:** point it to a WhatsApp chat or a booking page, not just your homepage.

Our [Google Business Profile guide](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-kerala/) covers each setting in detail.

## Step 2: Build a steady review routine

Ask every patient at the end of a successful visit, with the review link on WhatsApp the same day. Reply to every review, but never confirm in public that someone is your patient or discuss their treatment, even if they mention it. A simple "Thank you for your kind words, we're glad you're comfortable" protects privacy.

See [how to get more Google reviews within Google's rules](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews/) for the full routine.

## Step 3: A website that answers patient questions

Patients want to know four things: what a treatment involves, roughly what it costs, whether it hurts, and how to book. A simple website with one page per main treatment answers those questions and gives Google more reasons to show you for treatment searches.

Each treatment page should cover:

- What the treatment is, in plain language.
- Who it is for and how many visits it usually takes.
- A price range or "consultation required" if prices vary.
- Aftercare basics.
- A clear WhatsApp and call button.

## Step 4: Ads, used carefully

Paid ads can bring in patients for planned treatments such as aligners, implants or family check-ups. Two sets of rules apply.

**Professional rules.** Dentists in India are governed by the Dental Council of India's code of ethics, which restricts how practitioners may advertise. Before running any campaign, check the current rules with your council or advisor. In practice, most clinics stay safe by:

- Avoiding claims like "best dentist in Kerala" or "painless guaranteed".
- Not promising outcomes or using dramatic before-and-after images.
- Focusing on information: services, timings, location, technology and how to book.

**Platform rules.** Meta and Google both restrict health-related advertising. Meta does not allow you to target people by health conditions, and ads must not imply that you know a viewer's condition ("Are your teeth yellow?"). Google Ads applies its own healthcare policies. Ads built around location, family audiences and clear service information generally work best.

## Step 5: Make booking easy and track it

Most Kerala patients prefer WhatsApp to forms. Use a WhatsApp Business number with quick replies for common questions (timings, location, first-visit fee), and label each chat by where it came from: Google Maps, website, Instagram, or ad. At the end of each month you will know which channel brings real appointments, not just messages. ## Frequently asked questions

### Can dental clinics run Facebook and Instagram ads?

Yes, within Meta's health advertising policies and your professional council's rules. Ads should inform about services, location and booking, without targeting health conditions or promising results.

### Should a dental clinic show treatment prices online?

Showing a price range or a "starting from" figure for common treatments reduces time-wasting calls and builds trust. For complex treatments, say that a consultation is needed for an exact quote.

### How long does it take for a clinic to rank on Google Maps?

A complete, verified profile can start appearing within weeks. Moving into the top three for competitive searches usually takes several months of steady reviews, photos and activity.

### Can I reply to a patient's review about their treatment?

Reply, but do not confirm they are a patient or discuss any treatment details. Keep replies general and offer to talk by phone if there is a concern.
