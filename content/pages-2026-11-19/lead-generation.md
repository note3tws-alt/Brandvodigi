## A lead is only worth something if it turns into a customer

Most businesses that say "our leads are bad" are right, and the cause is usually one of four things:

1. **The offer is vague.** "Contact us for details" gets curious clicks. "Free skin consultation this week, 12 slots" gets bookings.
2. **The form is too easy.** No questions means anyone can submit, including people with no budget.
3. **Nobody replies fast.** A lead that waits until tomorrow has usually already called a competitor.
4. **No one counts what happened next.** Without knowing which leads became customers, budget goes to the cheapest leads instead of the best ones.

Lead generation, done properly, fixes all four.

## What we build

### The offer
We work out what will make your ideal customer act now: a consultation, a site visit, a trial class, a festival package or a free estimate. Then we write it plainly, with the price range where that helps.

### The capture route
- **Meta instant forms** with qualifying questions (budget, location, timeline) for volume.
- **Click-to-WhatsApp ads** for businesses where conversation sells, which is most of Kerala.
- **Landing pages** for higher-value services where people want detail before they enquire. See [website design](/services/website-design/).
- **Google Business Profile** calls and messages for people searching nearby. See [local SEO](/services/local-seo/).

### The follow-up system
A shared lead sheet or simple CRM, WhatsApp quick replies, a same-day call routine and reminders for leads who said "later". This is often where the biggest gains are, and it costs nothing in ad spend.

### The tracking
Every lead tagged by campaign and ad, with a column for "became a customer", so we can see the real cost per sale and move budget to what works. See [performance marketing](/services/performance-marketing/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between lead generation and performance marketing?
Performance marketing is the wider practice of running paid media against business results. Lead generation is the part focused on enquiries: the offer, the capture route and the follow-up for businesses that sell through conversations rather than online checkouts.

### Do you sell leads?
No. We run campaigns in your own ad account and send enquiries straight to you, so the leads, data and audiences stay yours.

### How much does a lead cost in Kerala?
It varies widely by industry, offer and location. A salon booking and a villa enquiry are very different. We set a target from your numbers after the first test month rather than quoting a figure in advance.

### Can leads come straight to WhatsApp?
Yes. Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a chat with your business number, and we set up quick replies so your team can respond fast.

### How soon will I get leads?
Usually within the first week of a campaign going live. Getting the cost per customer stable takes a few weeks of testing.
