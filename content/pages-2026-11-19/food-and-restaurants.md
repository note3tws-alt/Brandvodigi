## How people choose where to eat or order

- **They see it first on Instagram.** A Reel of a cake being cut or a biriyani pot being opened does more than any menu card.
- **They check Google Maps.** Rating, review count, photos and opening hours decide whether they walk in.
- **They decide fast.** Birthday cakes, office lunches and weekend dinners are often decided the same day.
- **They order on WhatsApp.** Especially for custom cakes, bulk orders and catering.

So food marketing has to be visual, local, fast and easy to act on.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Instagram marketing work for restaurants in Kerala?
Yes, when content is shot well, posted consistently and paired with an easy way to order or book. Reels of food being prepared or served tend to reach far more new people than static menu posts.

### Should a small bakery run ads?
Usually yes, once the page has good content to show. Small, local budgets aimed at people nearby, with WhatsApp ordering, can pay for themselves quickly. We start small and scale on results.

### Are Zomato and Swiggy enough?
They bring orders but take a commission and own the customer relationship. Your own Instagram, Google profile and WhatsApp let regulars order directly.

### How do I get more Google reviews for my restaurant?
Ask at the right moment (when the bill is paid or the order is delivered), make it one tap with a QR code or link, and reply to every review.

### Do you help with food photos and videos?
We plan the shoot, guide the styling with your team and edit the footage for Reels and ads.
