Every industry buys differently. A villa buyer takes months and asks the family in Dubai; a cake buyer decides tonight. Brandvo Digital Co. plans **digital marketing for Kerala businesses** around how your customers actually decide, using what we have learned from clients in each sector.

## [Real estate](/industries/real-estate/)
Qualified buyer leads for builders, brokers and land sellers: qualifying forms, walkthrough Reels, NRI targeting and fast WhatsApp follow-up. Clients include SAJ Realty and Land4Sale Payyanur.

## [Healthcare, clinics and hospitals](/industries/healthcare/)
Patient enquiries and bookings for clinics, dental practices and hospitals, with doctor-led content, Google Maps visibility and appointment flows, within medical advertising rules.

## [Beauty and salons](/industries/beauty-and-salons/)
Bookings for salons, spas, bridal studios and designer lounges, with before-and-after content, offers and reviews. Clients include Ruyah by Naas, Kozhikode.

## [Food and restaurants](/industries/food-and-restaurants/)
More orders and footfall for bakeries, restaurants, cafés and cloud kitchens, with food content, local creators and WhatsApp ordering. See how [Unicorn Bakes](/work/unicorn-bakes/) made ₹95 lakh in sales from ₹8 lakh in ad spend.
