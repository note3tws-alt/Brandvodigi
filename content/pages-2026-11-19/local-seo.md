## Why the map pack matters

Search "dentist in Payyanur" or "bakery near me" and Google shows a map with three businesses before any website. Those three get most of the calls, direction requests and website visits. Which three appear depends mainly on:

- **Relevance:** how well your Google Business Profile matches the search (categories, services, description).
- **Distance:** how close you are to the person searching.
- **Prominence:** reviews, ratings, photos, links and mentions across the web.

You can't change your distance. You can change everything else, and most local competitors in Kerala do very little of it.

## What our local SEO service includes

### Google Business Profile set-up and management
Correct name, address and phone; the right primary and secondary categories; services and products listed; a description that sounds like you; real photos of your shop, team and work; and weekly posts with offers and updates. We also answer questions on the profile and keep holiday hours right.

### A review system that runs every week
Most happy customers will leave a review if you ask at the right time and make it easy. We set up a short link and QR code, a WhatsApp message your team can send after each sale or visit, and a reply routine. We never buy reviews or offer rewards for them, because that breaks Google's rules and can get the profile suspended.

### Citations and NAP consistency
Your business name, address and phone (NAP) listed the same way on Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Facebook and relevant industry directories. Mismatched details confuse Google and customers.

### Location pages that are actually useful
A page for each area you genuinely serve, with local details, services, proof and FAQs. No copy-paste pages that only swap the town name.

### Local links and mentions
Local associations, event sponsorships, supplier and partner pages, and local news where you have a real story.

### Reporting
Calls, direction requests, website clicks and searches from your profile each month, plus where you appear in the map for your main searches.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between SEO and local SEO?
SEO covers your website's rankings in normal search results. Local SEO focuses on Google Maps and local results: your Business Profile, reviews, citations and location signals. Most local businesses need both, but local SEO usually brings calls faster.

### Can you rank my business in a town where I have no office?
Google Maps rankings are tied to your verified address. We will not create fake addresses. For towns you serve but are not based in, we use service-area settings, useful location pages and ads instead.

### How do I get more Google reviews?
Ask every satisfied customer at the right moment, make it one tap with a short link or QR code, and reply to every review. We set up the whole routine for your team.

### Is a Google Business Profile free?
Yes. The profile itself is free; what takes time is setting it up properly and keeping it active.

### Do you fix duplicate or suspended profiles?
We can help merge duplicates and prepare a reinstatement request for a suspended profile when the business is genuine and the details are correct.
